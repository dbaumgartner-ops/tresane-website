How We Work

The Tresane Model®

There is no universal playbook. But there is a clearer way of seeing leadership. We work across three domains — because alignment in all three is where real change holds.

01

Align Self

Clarity of values, identity, and purpose. Leaders who know what grounds them can hold pressure without being consumed by it.

02

Align Relationships

Respect, curiosity, and trust. The quality of how a leader connects with others determines how much of the work moves without them pushing it.

03

Align Teams

Shared purpose, systems, and accountability. Teams that own the work together free the leader to lead instead of manage every detail.

WeQ Assessment

How well does your team think together?

The WeQ — Workplace Effectiveness Quotient — measures collective intelligence across your team. Not individual performance. The system the team creates together.

Leaders who take the WeQ gain a clear picture of where alignment is strong, where it is creating drag, and what a few targeted shifts could unlock.
